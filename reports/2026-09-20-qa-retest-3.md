## Finding 10 (new) — a failed save leaves one day's text in another day's editor, then saves it there

**Severity:** High · `state.js` `syncWeekDataWithApi`, `notes.js` `renderNotes`

The guards added for Finding 06 key off a single **global** flag, `STATE.isNotesDirty`, not the
week/day being edited. It is now cleared only *after* the save response arrives (previously before
the request), and never cleared after a failure. While it is set, both `syncWeekDataWithApi`
(skips assigning the server's `noteSheets`) and `renderNotes` (skips updating the textarea) do
nothing — for **any** week, not just the one that was being edited. Verified live:
```
D4  save fails on day A, then navigate to day B (server still failing):
    editor on B shows "ONLY FOR DAY A"                          (expected "")
D4b after the server recovers and the page is reloaded:
    day B has "ONLY FOR DAY A" (expected "")
    day A has ""               (expected "ONLY FOR DAY A")
```
Day A's note is moved to day B, and day A is left empty. The same mechanism explains the single
remaining zero-wait loss in the 15-run diagnostic: `iter 3: A="Day B entry" B=""` — day B's text
appeared on day A. Because the flag is also held for the whole in-flight window of *every* save,
this is reachable without any failure, just by switching days while a save is in flight.
Real-world trigger: any transient network blip or 5xx while editing notes, followed by navigating.
Marked with an expected-failure test (`e2e/notes-save-behavior.spec.ts`).

**Suggestion:** scope the dirty state to the week/sheet being edited (e.g. store the dirty
`weekKey`+sheet id, and only skip the sync/render when the *current* week+sheet matches); flush or
discard the dirty edit for the outgoing week before switching; do not skip rendering when the week
has changed.

## Finding 11 (new) — regression: type in one sheet, switch to another, type, reload → wrong content

**Severity:** High · same guards (hypothesis; not root-caused to a line)

`e2e/note-sheets.spec.ts`'s custom-sheet test (type on the journal sheet, switch to a new custom
sheet, type there, blur, reload, reopen the custom sheet) passed on every earlier pin this session.
On `63498c7` it fails on its **first attempt in both full runs** and in **3 of 6** isolated
zero-retry runs, always the same way:
```
Expected: "Went well: shipped on time"     Received: "Journal entry before switching"
```
i.e. after reload the custom sheet shows the *journal* sheet's text. It passes only on Playwright's
retry (reported as "flaky"). This is ordinary usage — edit a sheet, click another sheet tab, edit,
reload — not a rapid-navigation edge case. The likely mechanism is the same global dirty flag
suppressing a render/sync at the moment of the tab switch (hypothesis — QA has not traced the line).
Nothing was changed in the test; it is doing its job.

## Other observations

- The reply says the fix was verified with the exact zero-wait sequence and "Day A's text is 100%
  retained". QA's 15 runs of that sequence found 1 loss, and the loss was of the Finding 10 kind.
  Worth re-running that sequence many times rather than once.
- `document.activeElement === textarea` is treated as "unsaved edits" in `syncWeekDataWithApi`, so a
  merely *focused* (untouched) editor also blocks loading the server's notes for that sync.
